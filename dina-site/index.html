The challenge

Develop a national tourism brand concept for Kazakhstan and present it to senior state stakeholders, while creating a modern story for an international audience.

The approach & proof

In collaboration with Media Gurus Inc., I worked on the brand concept and strategic narrative and wrote the script for a national promotional video broadcast on Euronews. Inform.kz coverage ↗ · Sostav.ru ↗

Teach for Qazaqstan — Brand Strategy

The challenge

Give an international social and education movement actionable tools for clear communication as it entered Kazakhstan.

The approach

Qualitative research and interviews across international program branches, including participants from Bangladesh, Russia and London, translated into local communication implications.

Esil University — Rebranding & Brand Strategy

The challenge

Refresh perception of the former KazUEFMT and create a stronger platform for communication.

The approach

Market research, competitor and trend analysis, international benchmarks, brand platform, naming options, slogans, audience segmentation, messaging and SMM guidance.

TROYA / volson.kz — Communication Strategy

The challenge

Build a new office and educational furniture brand for Kazakhstan with future export ambitions.

The approach

Market and competitor research, customer touchpoints, brand platform, naming and slogans, with visual identity developed in collaboration with Appazov Branding Agency.
